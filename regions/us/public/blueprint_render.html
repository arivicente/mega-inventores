HYDRAULIC CRANE (PROJECT #04)

LEVEL 2 • TIME: 35 MIN
🏗️ Live Working Model 💧 Water-Powered

⚙️ Real student build made entirely from recycled cardboard & syringes

📦 Materials Needed (100% Household Items):

Cardboard Box • 2x 10ml Plastic Syringes • Aquarium Tube • Bamboo Skewer • Craft String

STEP 01

Cut base arm and reinforce the pivot joint with craft washers.

STEP 02

Mount the hydraulic syringe cylinder and connect the water tube.

STEP 03

Test the lifting mechanism and calibrate counterweight balance.

💡

STEM Physics Insight: Fluid Pressure Principle (Pascal's Law) multiplies lifting force when water pushes through closed tubes.
